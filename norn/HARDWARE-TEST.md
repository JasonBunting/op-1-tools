# Norn: hardware check (0.5.1)

0.5.0 adds the screen, with simple placeholder figures until the real ones arrive. The sound is unchanged. Install
`build/norn.knl` and open Norn's engine screen (T1).

1. Look at the top strip.
   - Expect: MODEL / SHAPER (all in capitals, no icons or question marks) in blue and white, with the first of four short segments lit; CURVE, FOLD and SLOPE with
     percentages and a track each (ends and centre marked, a coloured marker at the setting). Nothing cut off.
2. Turn green, white and orange slowly, one at a time.
   - Expect: the value and marker follow; the threads reshape smoothly; a ring and a letter (H, T or M) flash at that
     knob's hand and fade in about a second.
3. Turn blue through all four models.
   - Expect: the labels change (2-OP FM: RATIO, DEPTH, FEEDBACK; FORMANT: INTERVAL, FORMANT, WIDTH; TERRAIN: TERRAIN,
     RADIUS, OFFSET) and the weave's pattern with them. On 2-OP FM, orange at its centre reads OFF.
4. Hold a few notes, then let go.
   - Expect: small bright dots run along the threads and the threads sway slightly; after you let go the dots fade
     within a second. Nothing stays lit.
5. On T4, put an LFO on orange, then come back to T1.
   - Expect: a small hollow square under orange's track moving with the LFO, the solid marker staying put, the weave
     moving.
6. With the screen showing, the heaviest model (wave terrain, green about 60%, white right, orange middle), open the
   monitor and read **synth** and **pk** with six keys held, as before.
   - Expect: about the same as before (31 / 59). A screenshot from OP-1 Mirror would help too.

Report anything that looks wrong, plus the two numbers.
