# Norn

A synth engine for the original OP-1 running Hundekraft 3.3.2, built from the synthesis models of Mutable
Instruments' [Plaits](https://pichenettes.github.io/mutable-instruments-documentation/modules/plaits/manual/). Not a
Mutable Instruments product.

**Status: prototype 0.4.1.** All four planned models, chosen with blue: the waveshaper (31%), two-operator FM (24% of the
CPU for six voices), the formant model (25%), and wave terrain (new in 0.4.0, 31%). The plugin is
68 KB, most of it Plaits' wavetables for three of wave terrain's terrains.

## Controls

Blue chooses the **model** in four fixed quarters: waveshaper, FM, formant, wave terrain.

| Knob | Waveshaper (first quarter) | FM (second quarter) | Formant (third quarter) | Wave terrain (last quarter) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Green | **Harmonics:** the waveshaper curve. Centre is none; left and right bend the wave in different ways | **Harmonics:** the frequency ratio of the two sines, stepping through musical ratios (left: below the note, right: up to 8 times it) | **Harmonics:** the second formant, 2 octaves below the first (left) to 2 above (right); left of centre the note's own buzz also comes through | **Harmonics:** the terrain, morphing through eight: five made of formulas, then three made of Plaits' wavetables |
| White | **Timbre:** the wavefolder. Further right folds the wave back on itself, brighter and buzzier | **Timbre:** how hard one sine bends the other, from a pure tone to bright and clangy | **Timbre:** the formant, low to high (33 Hz to 4.2 kHz), the same whatever note you play: vowel-like sweeps | **Timbre:** the size of the circle traced over the terrain, small and soft (left) to large and rich (right); smaller on high notes |
| Orange | **Morph:** the wave's slope, a triangle at the left to a ramp at the right | **Morph:** feedback. Centre is none; left roughens the pitch, right adds grit, both noisy at the ends | **Morph:** the pulse that carries the formant, narrow and thin (left) to wide and full (right); low notes only, higher ones keep a middle shape | **Morph:** where the circle sits on the terrain, sliding it across (and shrinking it towards the edges) |

The envelope (T2), effects (T3) and LFO (T4) work as with any synth. There is no screen yet: the OP-1 shows its four
bars.

## Files

| Path | What |
| --- | --- |
| `plugin/norn.c` | The engine: the model choice, knobs, notes, velocity. |
| `plugin/ws_dsp.h` | The waveshaper in integers, ported from Plaits; its header lists every change from Plaits. |
| `plugin/fm_dsp.h` | The two-operator FM in integers, ported from Plaits; its header lists every change from Plaits. |
| `plugin/gr_dsp.h` | The formant model (Plaits' granular formant oscillator) in integers; its header lists every change. |
| `plugin/wt_dsp.h` | Wave terrain in integers, ported from Plaits; its header lists every change. |
| `plugin/ws_tables.h`, `plugin/fm_tables.h`, `plugin/gr_tables.h`, `plugin/wt_tables.h` | The curves, FM ratios, note steps, terrain quotients and wavetables, generated by `plugin/gen_tables.py` (mostly from Plaits' `resources.cc`). |
| `plugin/norn_level.h` | The voice level. |
| `plugin/test/host_test.c` | Host test of every model: pitch, the start of a note, every knob corner, level, block edges, a steal; writes WAVs. |
| `plugin/test/ref_compare.cc` | Compares the port with Plaits' own engines by spectrum, 375 settings a model. |
| `HARDWARE-TEST.md` | The device test. |

## Build and test

Build with `tools/build.sh norn` (makes `build/norn.knl`). Tests, in WSL from the repository root (WAVs go to
`build/`):

```bash
gcc -O2 -Wall -I norn/plugin norn/plugin/test/host_test.c -lm -o /tmp/norn_test && /tmp/norn_test build
```

The comparison with Plaits needs the Mutable Instruments source (`MI`, here a WSL path):

```bash
MI=/mnt/d/src/mutable-instruments
g++ -O2 -Wall -I norn/plugin -I $MI norn/plugin/test/ref_compare.cc $MI/plaits/dsp/engine/waveshaping_engine.cc \
    $MI/plaits/dsp/engine/fm_engine.cc $MI/plaits/dsp/engine/grain_engine.cc \
    $MI/plaits/dsp/engine2/wave_terrain_engine.cc $MI/plaits/resources.cc $MI/stmlib/dsp/units.cc -o /tmp/norn_ref
/tmp/norn_ref && /tmp/norn_ref fm && /tmp/norn_ref gr && /tmp/norn_ref wt
```

FM has one known miss: a noisy setting (C2, harmonics 0.25, timbre 0.75, morph 0) whose band levels differ by about
1.6 dB, just outside the test's tolerance. Wave terrain's high notes (72 and up) fold harmonics back from far above
the sample rate, differently at Plaits' rate and the OP-1's, so they get a wider tolerance; `REF_SELF=1 /tmp/norn_ref
wt` compares Plaits with itself at the OP-1's rate, which differs by more than the port does.
`/tmp/norn_ref 60 0.75 0.5 0` (note, harmonics, timbre, morph; `fm`, `gr` or `wt` first for the other models) prints
one setting's spectrum. Regenerate the tables with
`python norn/plugin/gen_tables.py <MI>/plaits/resources.cc`.
